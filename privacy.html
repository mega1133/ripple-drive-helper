Privacy policy

Ripple Drive Helper. Last updated October 27, 2026.

Who uses it

This is a private internal tool used only by Josh Liu and Ryan Wang of Ripple. It is not available to the public.

What it accesses

With permission, it reads and edits Google Drive files (Docs, Sheets and Slides) in the account of the person who signed in. It only works on files that person can already open.

Where data is kept

The login token is stored only on our own computer. File contents are used only to do the task that was asked for and are not stored on any server we run.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. We do not use it for ads. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions go to Josh Liu or Ryan Wang directly.
