Ripple Drive Helper

Ripple Drive Helper is a private internal tool for Ripple, run by Josh Liu and Ryan Wang. It is not offered to the public.

It lets our own assistant read and edit our own Google Drive files (Docs, Sheets and Slides) so we can prepare client documents faster.

It only works on Google accounts that belong to Josh Liu or Ryan Wang. Login tokens are stored only on our own computer. Nothing is shared with anyone else.
